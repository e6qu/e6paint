e6paint

A canvas-first, multiplayer, deeply-zoomable whiteboard where code-drawn diagrams are first-class citizens.

Source (AGPL-3.0-or-later) Open full-screen Design

This browser does not expose WebGPU — e6paint renders through WebGPU and will not start here. Try a current Chrome, Edge, or Safari 26+.

Live build, embedded. Draw with the tool bar (digits 1–9 switch tools), double the fun with ?relay=https://your-relay:8480 in the address bar to point at a relay and share room links from the app's Share button. Your board autosaves in this browser.
